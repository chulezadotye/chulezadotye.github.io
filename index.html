Honours Research Project

A Comparative Study of Bio-inspired Optimization Algorithms for Improving Deep Learning Models in Breast Cancer Image Classification

Research area: Artificial Intelligence and Medical Image Classification

This project investigates Genetic Algorithms (GA), Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), and Differential Evolution (DE) for optimizing the learning rate and batch size of a convolutional neural network (CNN). The model is evaluated using breast cancer images to classify cases as benign or malignant.

Tools: Python, TensorFlow, CNNs, Jupyter Notebook

Evaluation: Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, training time, and statistical testing.
